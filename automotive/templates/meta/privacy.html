Who we are

{{ shop.name_en }} ({{ shop.name_ka }}) is a car service and car parts shop at {{ shop.address_en }}. Phone: {{ shop.phone }}.

This policy explains how we handle your data when you write to our Facebook page “{{ shop.page_name }}” on Messenger or comment on its posts.

What we collect

  • The messages you send to our page on Messenger, including photos, voice messages and other files;
  • comments you leave on our page’s posts and ads;
  • your name as Facebook shows it, and the ID number Meta assigns you for our page only (page-scoped ID);
  • which of our ads or links you contacted us from;
  • the replies we send you.

Why we use it

  • To answer your questions about parts, services, prices and stock;
  • to keep the conversation, so our staff can see it and continue it;
  • to see how our ads perform (for example, how many conversations each ad started).

We do not sell your data and do not use it to build an advertising profile.

Automatic replies

Some replies are written automatically by our assistant, an artificial intelligence program. It can make mistakes. Our staff see the conversations and can step in at any time. You can also call us: {{ shop.phone }}.

Who else processes your data

  • Meta Platforms, the company behind Facebook and Messenger, through which your messages reach us. It processes data under its own privacy policy.
  • OpenRouter and Anthropic (USA), the artificial intelligence service that writes the automatic replies. They receive the text of the conversation to write a reply and handle it under their own terms (OpenRouter, Anthropic).

We pass your data to no one else unless the law requires it.

Where and how long we keep it

We keep conversations in our own database in Georgia until you ask us to delete them. Technical copies of incoming messages are deleted within {{ shop.inbox_days }} days.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to us on Messenger or call {{ shop.phone }}. To have your data deleted, it is enough to write “delete my data”. Changes
